Clutch Kings Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clutch Kings: Esports Manager is a free single-player game. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not ask for your name, email or other personal details.

Data stored on your device

Your saves, settings and an optional manager photo you pick from your photo library are kept only on your device and are removed when you delete the game. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. We do not run our own servers and do not collect, share or sell personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
